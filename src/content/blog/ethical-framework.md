# on the topic of ethical representation.

<p class="subtitle">perhaps a shot in the dark</p>

A though occured to me recently about the work that I did in my
[previous research](/blog/tor-research).

Really, we can think of ethical representation in research as a function of some
space, some idealisation of ethical ideals, which we want to constrict in some
way.

For example, Bell-Lapadula constructs a space of the abstract idea of
"confidentiality" and aims to restrict that space via it's rules and
construction. We ensure a source that is confidential cannot be affected by one
that is less confidential. And further that someone without a level of
confidential access cannot access a confidential document.

Biba applies the same model for integrity, ensuring that a source with integrity
is not sullied by one with less integrity than itself.

Both of these models construct a "space" a dimension of ethical representation
that they wish to focus on, and restrict it with some ruleset.
