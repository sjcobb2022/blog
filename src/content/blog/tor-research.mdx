## why write this paper to begin with

The dark web is handled in a slightly paradoxical manner in contemporary
discourse. It is praised as a mechanism for protecting free speech and privacy,
while enabling serious criminal activity through those same mechanisms. As a
result, dark web research places researchers in close proximity to highly
sensitive material. Including but not limited to, personal disclosures, criminal
marketplaces, and media whose very possession may be illegal. Despite this,
existing work seems to offer over a limited insight into how researchers protect
themselves, their institutions and the people whose data they collect.

For the subsequent writing, we will refer to Tor as the source of dark web
material, due to it's mainstream nature.
